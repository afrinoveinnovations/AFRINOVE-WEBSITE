Case Studies

The Challenge Local farmers in the Zombo region lacked direct access to premium international markets, relying on intermediaries that significantly reduced their margins and stifled sustainable growth. Our Solution Afrinove architected a comprehensive digital supply chain transparency tool and provided strategic financial advisory to form a centralized cooperative. We implemented quality control frameworks (as seen in our 'Coffee Inspected' initiative) to meet global standards. The Result A 300% increase in direct-to-market exports within 18 months, securing premium pricing and directly impacting the livelihoods of over 5,000 regional farmers.
The Challenge A legacy regional bank struggled with outdated core banking systems, leading to high transaction failure rates and massive customer churn to agile mobile-first competitors. Our Solution We led a ground-up digital transformation initiative, integrating modern API gateways and establishing a cloud-native microservices architecture, without disrupting ongoing operations. The Result System uptime improved to 99.99%, transaction processing speed increased by 40x, and the bank successfully launched a new digital wallet product acquiring 100k users in Q1.
The Challenge High youth unemployment combined with aging farming demographics threatened long-term food security and economic stability in East Africa. Our Solution Afrinove partnered with NGOs to design and execute a scalable capacity-building program, focusing on modern agritech adoption, business management, and micro-financing for young agri-preneurs. The Result Successfully trained and funded over 500 youth-led agricultural startups, creating a ripple effect of over 2,000 secondary jobs in local communities.

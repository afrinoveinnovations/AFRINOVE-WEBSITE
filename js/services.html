Minerals & Mining Trading

Africa's Critical Minerals.
Global Markets.

Afrinove operates as principal trader and advisory intermediary in Africa's critical minerals sector — connecting verified producers to international buyers at premium valuations.

Flagship Vertical

Minerals & Mining
Trading

We facilitate procurement, quality assurance, documentation, and international sale of mineral concentrates — with full OECD due diligence compliance and escrow-secured settlement.

  • Copper Concentrate (25%–45% Cu)
  • Cobalt Hydroxide & Sulphate
  • Gold Doré & Refined Gold
  • Tantalum (Coltan) Ore
  • Lithium Spodumene Concentrate
  • Iron Ore & Manganese
  • Rare Earth Elements (REE)
Our Process
01
Source Verification
Due diligence on mining operations, legal title, and ESG compliance prior to engagement.
02
Assay & Quality Certification
Independent laboratory assay and third-party certification of mineral grade and volume.
03
Documentation & Compliance
Export permits, certificates of origin, OECD frameworks, and trade finance structuring.
04
Buyer Matching & Escrow
Direct connection to verified international smelters and refineries with escrow-secured settlement.
05
Logistics & Delivery
End-to-end freight management from mine gate to destination port, with full insurance coverage.
